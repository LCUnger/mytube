# MyTube privacy policy

MyTube stores your switch settings in the browser's local extension storage. Removing the extension removes these preferences. MyTube has no accounts, analytics, advertising, server, or remote filter downloads.

On YouTube pages the extension applies local display rules and checks video playback state. The browser evaluates bundled ad-blocking rules on your device. MyTube does not collect, retain, or transmit your browsing history, searches, video titles, account details, or page contents. It does not sell or share personal information.

YouTube and your browser process information under their own privacy policies.
