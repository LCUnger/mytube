# MyTube — YouTube focus controls

Keep your subscriptions and choose what else you see on YouTube. MyTube starts by filtering Shorts, community posts and polls, blocking identifiable ads, and stopping playback at the end of a video, including in playlists.

Use fifteen independent switches to hide recommendations, comments, Mixes, shopping offers and more. Keep the Home feed available or hide it entirely. Settings take effect in open YouTube tabs and stay on your device.

Ad coverage varies with YouTube's delivery method. MyTube is an independent project and has no affiliation with YouTube, Google, Microsoft or Unhook.
